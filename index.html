SilentIQ Privacy Policy

Effective 27 November 2026 · App: SilentIQ (com.nasir.silentiq) · Developer: Nasir Shaik

In short: SilentIQ has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your routines, places, settings and history are stored only on your phone. We, the developer, do not receive any of your data. The only time the app itself sends anything off your phone is the optional nearby-mosque lookup, described at the end of this policy.

What SilentIQ does

SilentIQ switches your phone to silent or vibrate during your work hours, classes, sleep or any routine you set up, during calendar meetings, and at places, Wi-Fi networks or Bluetooth devices you choose. It then restores your sound when that moment ends. An optional prayer-times feature is described separately below.

SilentIQ does not read your contacts, call log, messages, photos, files or microphone. Letting starred contacts or repeat callers ring through uses Android's own Do Not Disturb settings; the app never sees who is calling.

Special Android access

  • Do Not Disturb access: to turn silent and Do Not Disturb on and off.
  • Notifications: to tell you when quiet starts and ends, and when something needs your attention.
  • Alarms and reminders (exact alarms): so your quiet windows start and end on time.
  • Battery optimisation exemption and run at startup: so Android doesn't pause the app and your schedule keeps working after a restart.

Each of these is used only on your phone.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share your data. The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting libraries.

Backups and exports

SilentIQ turns off Android's automatic cloud backup, so your settings are not copied to Google Drive. If you move to a new phone using Android's phone-to-phone transfer, your settings can be copied directly to the new phone; they don't pass through us. You can choose to export your settings (and, if your phone's Developer options are turned on, the event log) to a file. The file goes wherever you save or send it.

Purchases

SilentIQ is free to try for 3 days. After that, a one-time purchase through Google Play unlocks it; there is no subscription. Payments, refunds and receipts are handled entirely by Google Play. The app learns only whether you have bought SilentIQ, never your name, card or payment details. Google's handling of your payment is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Deleting your data

All data is on your phone. Uninstalling SilentIQ, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, deletes it. There is nothing stored by us to delete. Google Play keeps its own record of your purchase so you can restore it on a new phone.

Children

SilentIQ is a general utility and is not directed at children under 13.

Optional prayer-times feature

SilentIQ includes an optional feature that quiets your phone during prayer times. If you don't use it, nothing in this section applies to you.

  • Prayer times are calculated on your phone from your location (approximate location is enough). Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Nearby mosque lookup: when you search for mosques, or turn on Quiet at mosques, SilentIQ sends the latitude and longitude of the location used for prayer times to a public OpenStreetMap Overpass server (overpass-api.de, or overpass.kumi.systems as a fallback) and receives a list of nearby mosques. While Quiet at mosques stays on, this lookup repeats about once a week. No name, account or device identifier is sent by the app. These servers are run by third parties under their own privacy policies; like any web server, they can see your IP address. Turning off Quiet at mosques stops these lookups.

Apart from this lookup and Google Play services (used for location and purchases), SilentIQ does not contact any server.
